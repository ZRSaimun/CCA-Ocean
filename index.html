WHY OBSERVATION MATTERS · 05A

Better decisions begin with better visibility.

Reef images are useful only when researchers can interpret what they contain. Underwater colour shifts, low contrast, blur and visually similar coral classes make that difficult. This is where the research question becomes practical: can computer vision preserve colour information and boundaries well enough to support finer-grained reef analysis?

RESEARCH ANCHOR · 07
CCA-NET / CORALSCAPES

A Colour-Constancy and Boundary-Aware Multi-Task Network for Fine-Grained Underwater Coral Reef Semantic Segmentation

Md Zillur Rahman Saimon · Pravallika Kondapalli · Sourav Das Polok · Muhammad Aumlanul Abrar

82.80%reported accuracy
58.0%reported mIoU

The abstract reports evaluation on CoralScapes against UNet++, DeepLabV3+, SegFormer and the SegFormer MiT-B2 baseline. It reports improved boundary detection and segmentation of visually similar coral types, with ablation evidence supporting UCN, BAR and hierarchical supervision.

This work is positioned as a monitoring and analysis tool. It does not directly stop bleaching, pollution or climate change; it aims to improve how underwater reef imagery can be interpreted.

FROM MODEL TO MISSION · 08A

AI does not save a reef.
Knowledge can help people act.

CCA-Net is a segmentation research approach, not a climate solution. Its potential contribution is narrower and useful: improve interpretation of difficult underwater coral imagery so monitoring and ecosystem assessment can become more informative.
